Hello, thank you for the review.

We fixed the order of the consent prompts in build 1.0.0 (4):

1. The GDPR consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is now shown first, only to users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.
2. The App Tracking Transparency request is shown only after that form has been closed. It is no longer requested by the native code at launch.
3. If the user selects "Ask App Not to Track", no further tracking request is shown in the same flow, and the ad SDK serves ads without the IDFA.

Outside the EEA/UK/Switzerland the GDPR form is not shown at all: users only see the App Tracking Transparency request. This regional difference is documented in the Review Notes.

Users can change their ad consent at any time from Settings > "Ad consent and privacy".

Thank you.
